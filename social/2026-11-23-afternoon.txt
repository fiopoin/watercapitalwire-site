Diamondback Energy and KKR are buying Five Point Infrastructure's 70% of Deep Blue Midland Basin, taking full ownership of a produced water network of more than 2,000 miles. Bloomberg put the equity value at about $2.1 billion. Closing is expected in the fourth quarter.
