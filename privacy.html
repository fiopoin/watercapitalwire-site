Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Water Capital Wire is free to read. You do not need an account, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information.

Analytics

We do not run analytics or tracking on this site. If we add visitor analytics, this page will say so first.

Tools on the page

The calculator, the Water Pulse self check and the poll run in your browser. The poll remembers your pick on your own device and sends nothing to us. The feedback box opens your own email app; we only see what you choose to send.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's note on data collection.
