How we work

Water Capital Wire is a free daily edition on the business of water, written for managers, investors and operators. It covers water across every industry: utilities, industry and manufacturing, energy and produced water, agriculture, mining, semiconductors and data centers, along with the capital, technology, regulation and security questions that run through all of them.

Down the pipe

Every lead story is traced through the water cycle: source, treatment, use, reuse and discharge, because a contract or a rule rarely stops at one stage.

Spotlights

Each week we profile a company, a leader, a chemicals or technology supplier, an innovation and an investor, from filings, company statements and dated reporting. Profiles are not endorsements.

Capital desk and watchlist

Deals are listed with the date, the parties and the value when it is disclosed, each linked to its source. The watchlist names the listed companies and funds we follow. We show a price only when a source gives a dated close.

The water atlas

Water stress, demand, infrastructure needs, emerging sectors and security data come from public sources such as the World Resources Institute, the United Nations, the US EPA and the World Bank. Each figure is labeled with its source and refreshed when new data is published.

Calculator and Water Pulse

The project calculator runs in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Its starting cases are illustrative inputs, not benchmarks. The Water Pulse self check and poll also run on your device only.

Standards

Every fact is linked to a dated source. We paraphrase and quote sparingly. Outlook lines are our reading and are labeled. Corrections are made in the archive. This is general information, not investment, legal or tax advice.
